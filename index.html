Privacy Policy for Pocket Dice Roller

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Summary: Pocket Dice Roller is a 100% offline utility application. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or non-personal user data, and the app requires zero device permissions.

1. Information Collection and Use

Pocket Dice Roller does not collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or location) or non-personal device data (such as IP addresses, device identifiers, or usage analytics).

All dice roll calculations and user interface interactions occur locally on your device in real time. No data is saved to external servers or cloud storage.

2. Device Permissions

Pocket Dice Roller operates with zero Android system permissions. Specifically:

  • No internet or network access permission is requested.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or location is requested.
  • Haptic feedback uses standard Android UI touch feedback APIs without requiring special hardware permissions.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

This application contains no advertisements and does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, crash-reporting tools, or tracking libraries.

4. Children's Privacy

Because Pocket Dice Roller does not collect any data or connect to the internet, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 or any other age group.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if new features are introduced. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
